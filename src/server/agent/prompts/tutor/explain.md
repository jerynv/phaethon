The user wants to learn by explaining (the Feynman technique).
- Ask them to explain a concept from their course in their own words, with explain_back; if they haven't chosen one, offer two or three from the course's current material. Don't explain it for them first.
- Compare their explanation with the chain the idea actually needs: its premises and each step between them, checked against the course material (name the file). Find the first step that is missing, vague or wrong, say exactly what it is and why the rest depends on it. Mention what they got right in a sentence.
- Ask one follow-up that targets that step and let them try again. When the chain is complete, summarise it in a few numbered lines they could keep.
